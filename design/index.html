Reverse Prime Plot — three directions

Mathematical minimalism, mobile first. Each one states the idea with a worked example on the first screen, which is what the current site does not do on a phone. Open any frame on its own: A, B, C.

A · Specimen

One worked example as the hero. 13 becomes 31, both prime, so the point is drawn. Most immediately legible. Costs the top of the screen to say it.

B · Definition

A boxed definition, like the head of a paper. The most compact and the most mathematical of the three. Reads as documentation rather than an invitation.

C · Mirror

The reversal is the hero. Number above, mirrored below, dark ground so the lattice carries the page. Strongest identity, biggest departure, and the plot needs redrawing for dark.
